# Post hoc validation (2026-11-09)

Additional validation of the manuscript's claims, run after the experiments E0–E4 and reported in Sections R10–R12 of `docs/extended_results.pdf`. The experiment code, stored results, and contracts were not modified; everything new is in this folder. No model was trained.

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `PREREG.md`, `PREREG.sha256` | Analysis plans and decision criteria fixed before execution, with the hash and time of recording |
| `DEVIATIONS.md` | Deviations from the plan, failed and aborted runs, excluded trials, and analyses added afterwards |
| `A_l3_power/` | (A) Power and decision reliability of the L3 equivalence test: re-analysis of the 190 E2 run logs, re-scoring of the 95 stored reference models, simulation of the rule's operating characteristics, and intervals at the nominal level (`code/a4_nominal_intervals.py`, added post hoc) |
| `B_c1_concurrency/` | (B) The acceptance race: contracts v3.1 and v3.2, unmodified, on a local EVM (six controlled orderings) and on Kaia Kairos (two concurrent submitters, 150 trials per version) |
| `C_independent_audit/` | (C) Independent re-derivation of the E3 and L3 claims from ledger events, the data split, stored checkpoints, and stored statistics, without the experiment's audit or summary code; `results/comparison.md` is the row-level table |
| `D_l1_reexec/` | (D, added 2026-11-10, own `PREREG.md`) Independent L1 re-execution of the 206 sampled type-B training units: units and commitments taken from the ledger's `AuditSampled` seeds and `RoundInputCommitted`/`UpdateCommitted` events, re-executed from the stored checkpoints in the pinned container; all 206 reproduced the committed update hashes (0 mismatches). The 877 type-A units cannot be re-executed (run folders not kept) |
| `E_typeA_regen/` | (E, added 2026-11-10, own `PREREG.md`) Deterministic regeneration of the 20 type-A training runs without the ledger (the run folders had been deleted after the experiment), compared round by round with the ledger's `RoundAggregated` hashes (1,000 of 1,000 match, start models 20 of 20, rejected units 20 of 20), followed by independent L1 re-execution of the 899 sampled units: 877 honest units passed, 22 injected units mismatched (T2a/T3/T10 by input root, T2b by output hash) |

Each experiment folder has `code/`, `logs/`, `results/`, and `REPORT.md`. The plan, the deviation log, and the reports are written in Korean; the extended results summarize them in English.

Notes:

- Local absolute paths in logs and result files were replaced by `<repo>` and `~`.
- The raw ledger responses used in (C) (about 51 MB) are not included; `C_independent_audit/code/c1_fetch_chain.py` fetches them again from the public RPC endpoint.
- Per-record statistics of (A) are stored outside the repository (`~/data/runs/validation/A2/`) and are regenerated by `A_l3_power/code/a2_reeval.py`.
- Kairos contracts deployed for (B): v3.1 `0xE24658be1BaC5Da3324FF56963D3791b1cBa1ABE`, v3.2 `0x8c7711ffECe146b486792a897b08754eFA6624E3`; pilot runs excluded from the analysis used `0x3fe79BE3EEDc9Ef2a8128Cd41FC65848652F38a4` (v3.1) and `0x3cac3f6AEf9567E85CE3cce13374B875f9e26279` (v3.2).
- The verifier of (C) was written in the authors' environment, so (C) is not a third-party reproduction.
