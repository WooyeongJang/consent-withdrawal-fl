# PTB-XL institution split

This folder fixes how PTB-XL v1.0.3 records are grouped into simulated institutions. Everything here was generated by `scripts/check_ptbxl_sites.py` and committed with hashes (`hashes.txt`) before any training. `summary.txt` is the generator's report, in Korean; this file is its English summary. **The split is a simulation built from the PTB-XL `site` field and should not be read as real multi-institution data.**

## Data

The data are 21,799 records from 18,869 patients. The training split (`strat_fold` 1–8) has 17,418 records, and validation and test (`strat_fold` 9 and 10) have 4,381. There are 51 sites, and 17 records have no site. The top three sites hold 93.2% of the records.

## Rules

1. **Data subject = patient.** All records of a `patient_id` stay together.
2. **Patients with records at several sites** (46 patients, 101 records) are assigned, with all their records, to the site where they have the most records. Ties go to the smaller site number. This moved 46 records away from their original site (`multi_site_patients.csv`).
3. **Records without a site** (17 records):
   - **3 records:** another record of the same patient has a site, so the record takes that site.
   - **11 training records:** no record of the patient has a site, so they are excluded from training (`excluded_records.csv`).
   - **3 validation/test records:** no record of the patient has a site. They are kept and have no institution.
4. **Sites without training patients** (site 49: 3 patients, folds 9–10 only) get no institution and are marked `unassigned`.
5. **Assigning sites to K institutions.** Sites are processed in descending order of training patients. Each site goes to the institution that has the fewest training patients so far. Ties go to the smaller site number, then the smaller institution number (`institution_map_K{3,5,10}.csv`, `patient_map_K{3,5,10}.csv`).
6. **Requirement.** Every institution's training set must contain all five diagnostic superclasses (NORM, MI, STTC, CD, HYP).
7. **Fixed evaluation folds.** `strat_fold` 1–8 are for training, 9 for validation, and 10 for test. Withdrawers are drawn only from training patients.

## Result (training set, after exclusions: 15,012 patients, 17,407 records)

These counts are before the superclass filter. The paper excludes the 411 records without a diagnostic superclass and therefore reports 14,812 patients and 17,073 records (6,275 / 4,812 / 3,725 patients per institution for K = 3) from the same assignment tables.

| K | Use in the paper | Training patients per institution | Max/min ratio | Min. positive records per institution and class | All five classes everywhere |

## Files

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `site_stats.csv` | Per original site: records, patients, evaluation share, class prevalence, Jensen–Shannon distance to the overall class mix |
| `multi_site_patients.csv` | Patients with records at several sites, and the assigned site |
| `excluded_records.csv` | Training records excluded because no record of the patient has a site |
| `institution_map_K{3,5,10}.csv` | site → institution |
| `patient_map_K{3,5,10}.csv` | patient_id, strat_fold, site, institution |
| `institution_train_K{3,5,10}.csv` | Training patients, records, and positive records per class for each institution |
| `summary.txt` | Generator report (Korean) |
| `hashes.txt` | SHA-256 of the exclusion list and the maps |

`patient_id` and `ecg_id` are the pseudonymous identifiers published with PTB-XL (CC BY 4.0).
